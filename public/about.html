About us

The analysis is automated. The judgement stays yours.

Fin Advisor is a co-pilot for SEBI-registered Investment Advisers. It takes care of the number-crunching and the document-reading, so the adviser can spend more time with the client.

Why we built it

Good advice takes hours of groundwork.

Before an adviser can recommend anything, they have to profile the client's risk, work out what each goal will cost, choose an asset mix, screen funds, read factsheets and offer documents, and check that the whole plan suits the client. Much of that work is repeated for every client and is easy to get wrong.

Fin Advisor does that groundwork in one place. After signing in, the adviser adds each client once. The co-pilot then produces a risk profile, goal-based plans, a fund shortlist with ten-year backtests, answers cited to the source documents, and a suitability check. All of it goes to the adviser to review.

What we believe

Three principles behind every screen.

They decide what the product will do, and what it won't.

The adviser decides

Nothing reaches a client until a registered adviser approves it. The AI drafts plans; it never sends them.

Every figure has a source

Numbers come from the planning engines, AMFI data or official documents, with a link to the page they came from. The language model doesn't make them up.

Every decision is recorded

Drafts, overrides, approvals and rejections are written to an audit log, signed with the adviser's name and registration number.

Who it's for

Registered advisers, not the public.

Fin Advisor is a professional tool for SEBI-registered Investment Advisers and their teams. It is not a robo-adviser, and it does not give investment advice to members of the public.

Only a signed-in adviser can add clients, and every plan stays inside the adviser's workspace until they approve it.
