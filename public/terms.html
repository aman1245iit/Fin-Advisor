1. The service

Fin Advisor is software that helps investment advisers analyse a client's situation. It profiles risk, builds goal-based mutual fund plans, screens direct-plan funds, backtests portfolios, answers research questions from official documents and runs suitability checks. It produces drafts for an adviser to review. Nothing is shared with a client unless an adviser approves it.

2. Not investment advice

Fin Advisor is not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser and does not give investment advice to anyone. Its outputs are analytical aids. Any advice a client receives is given by their adviser, under the adviser's own registration and responsibility.

Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Read all scheme-related documents carefully. Past performance and backtested results do not guarantee future returns. Projections and Monte Carlo probabilities are estimates based on assumptions, not promises.
